Featured Case Study — Beauty & Hair Care

Big Wura Hair Care

The Challenge

Big Wura Hair Care needed a way to introduce their products to new customers online — something more credible than a social page, and easier to browse than a DM conversation. Without a proper website, people had no single place to see the full range, understand the brand, or feel confident before reaching out.

The Solution

We designed and built a clean, premium storefront that puts the product line front and center. Every decision — from navigation to how each product is presented — was made to remove friction, so customers could browse, understand what they're buying, and reach out with confidence.

The Outcome

Big Wura Hair Care now has a website that matches the quality of their products — a stronger first impression, a more professional presence online, and something the team is genuinely proud to send to every new customer.
